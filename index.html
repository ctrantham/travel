On the mobile/express checkout: Universal's on-site hotels, Helios Grand included, let you check out through the Universal Orlando app or the in-room TV — you settle the bill and get your final folio emailed without a stop at the front desk. Since you won't be circling back to Helios that morning, that's what makes an 8:45a departure realistic: check out from the room, then head straight out with your bags rather than waiting in a lobby line. Two ways to handle the bags themselves: (1) simplest — just take them with you, riding along in the trunk for both legs (Helios → Fort Wilderness → MCO), since with rideshare rather than your own car there's nowhere to stash them in between; or (2) drop them with Bell Services / Luggage Storage at Helios on your way out (usually free, no time limit) and swing back through the resort on your way to MCO to collect them — adds a short detour and another rideshare leg, but means riding the horses without hauling suitcases. Given the tight-but-workable window, option 1 is probably the lower-hassle call.
Riders: Christa Trantham & Mike Crow, both listed 18+ on the confirmation. Note the ride's own fine print: if you ever arrive by Disney boat/bus instead of rideshare, budget 1.5 hours of transportation time to reach the Trail Ride Check-In building near the Outpost Depot — rideshare direct to Fort Wilderness avoids that entirely.
