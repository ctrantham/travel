The plan — day by day

Confirmed bookings are tagged confirmed; everything else is a suggestion, sequenced against the crowd calendar so the flexible days land on the lightest crowds. This is background reference — the day-of details live in Hour by hour below.

Sun, Sep 13 suggested MK crowd 1/10
Touch down & check into Bay Lake Tower

Flight lands 5:22 PM (AA, non-stop from DFW) — by the time you've got bags and a rideshare to Bay Lake Tower, you're realistically settling in around 6:30–7:00 PM. Magic Kingdom is technically at its lightest crowd of the whole trip today — but it's also hosting the ticketed Halloween Party tonight, so the park closes to regular guests around 6 PM, and you'll be landing right around then anyway. Easiest call: keep arrival day low-key with a later dinner at Disney Springs (no ticket needed) rather than racing the clock into any park.

Disney Springs dinner, three options that aren't Rainforest Cafe or T-Rex: Chef Art Smith's Homecomin' (Southern comfort food, famous fried chicken and cheddar biscuits), The Edison (industrial-1920s vibe, modern American food and specialty cocktails), or Wine Bar George (huge wine list with small plates and charcuterie) — all three take reservations on the Disney app.
Checked the park pass system — no park reservation is required for any of the four gates on 9/13 (confirmed the same for 9/14 and 9/15 too), so if arrival timing works out and you want to swing into a park today after all, nothing needs to be booked ahead for it.
MK fireworks from Bay Lake Tower: the regular nightly show, Happily Ever After, is currently running at 9:30 PM (shifting to 8:00 PM starting Nov 1) — but 9/13 is a Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party night, so that regular show doesn't go up; the party's own fireworks — Disney's Not-So-Spooky Spectacular — fire around 10:00 PM for wristband holders. Either way, no ticket is needed to catch a glimpse from home base — MK fireworks are visible from Bay Lake Tower's upper floors, the rooftop terrace, and the beach/pool area most nights, so it's worth a look outside around 9 PM.
Best seat in the house — Top of the World Lounge: Bay Lake Tower’s rooftop lounge is DVC-member-exclusive (and you’re staying on points), with a direct line of sight to Magic Kingdom. It’s the easiest place to catch tonight’s fireworks — just an elevator ride up. If you’d rather stay out, the classic monorail bar crawl is right here too: hop the resort loop from the Contemporary to the Grand Floridian and Polynesian lounges and back, an easy no-car evening.
Now that the flight's confirmed, the earlier 12:30 PM horseback riding slot is off the table — you're still in the air. It's now booked for Saturday morning instead — see Sep 19 below.
Mon, Sep 14 suggested EPCOT crowd 2/10
Animal Kingdom rope drop, then EPCOT
New plan — Animal Kingdom first: rope-drop AK for the three headliners (Avatar Flight of Passage, Na’vi River Journey, Expedition Everest), which knocks them out in the first hour with early entry at 7:30a. Then take the Disney bus from AK to Disney’s Riviera Resort and ride the Skyliner one stop to EPCOT’s International Gateway — the back entrance drops you straight into World Showcase for the Food & Wine + Lightning Lane afternoon below (Disney buses only reach EPCOT’s main front entrance, not the Gateway, which is why the Riviera Skyliner leg closes the gap). Bus is the only Disney transport to AK, so budget ~30–40 min getting there from Bay Lake Tower, then another ~15–20 min for the AK→Riviera bus and Skyliner hop.

EPCOT's lightest day of the whole trip, and it's bookended with resort-guest perks on both ends: Early Theme Park Entry gets you in at 8:30 AM (30 min ahead of the public 9 AM open), and Bay Lake Tower's DVC status also unlocks Extended Evening Theme Park Hours tonight, 9–11 PM. The optimized touring plan actually leans into how light the crowds are rather than rope-dropping: a late-morning start with a Food & Wine Festival grazing stop, Voices of Liberty at the American Adventure, then the afternoon headliners back-to-back on Lightning Lane — Soarin' Across America, Spaceship Earth, Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind, Mission: SPACE (Green track), and Test Track — before a second Food & Wine pass and Luminous: The Symphony of Us at 9 PM closes out the night, with the Extended Evening Hours there as a bonus if there's steam left after.

For plotting the grazing crawl: The Disney Food Blog's full booth-by-booth reviews rank every dish at every Food & Wine marketplace — the closest thing to a "best of" cheat sheet for the festival.
EPCOT fireworks: Luminous: The Symphony of Us is the nighttime spectacular over World Showcase Lagoon. It generally runs right around closing — with EPCOT open until 9 PM on the 14th, expect it around 9:00 PM, but double-check the exact minute in My Disney Experience that morning since Disney nudges it seasonally.
Lightning Lane purchased: Multi Pass covers Soarin' Across America (2:25–3:25 PM), Spaceship Earth (3:25–4:25 PM), and Test Track (4:45–5:45 PM); Single Pass covers Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind (3:25–4:25 PM).
Tue, Sep 15 suggested confirmed MK crowd 1/10
Magic Kingdom by day, Level 99 by night

MK's crowd number is the single lowest anywhere on the calendar this week. Same catch as Sunday: the Halloween Party has the park closing to day guests around 6 PM, so it's a shorter day than usual, but a genuinely empty one. The re-optimized touring plan front-loads early entry with Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, then Peter Pan's Flight, The Haunted Mansion, Jungle Cruise, Pirates of the Caribbean, the freshly reopened Big Thunder Mountain Railroad (new Rainbow Caverns scene), and Tiana's Bayou Adventure — nearly the whole ride list knocked out by late morning. A single Lightning Lane pass is booked for TRON Lightcycle/Run (11:25–12:25 PM, $19) right before your 12:20 PM lunch at The Beak and Barrel (Adventureland's pirate-themed lounge, confirmation #356252122317, 2 guests). The afternoon is easy: the Disney Festival of Fantasy Parade at 2 PM, The Hall of Presidents, Mad Tea Party, and three laps on the reimagined Buzz Lightyear's Space Ranger Spin — everything wrapped by around 4 PM, and this time the early closure works in your favor.

Correction on Disney Starlight: Dream the Night Away: it won't actually run on 9/15. Disney has confirmed Sept 15 as one of this month's Halloween Party dates, and on those nights both Starlight and the regular fireworks (Happily Ever After) are pulled from the schedule entirely — not delayed to the afternoon, just off — replaced by the party's own ticketed entertainment (Mickey's Boo-To-You parade, Not-So-Spooky Spectacular) for wristband holders only. Since 9/13 is also a party night, neither of your two Magic Kingdom days this trip will have Starlight or Happily Ever After running for day guests — worth knowing going in so it's not a surprise.
Three attractions are closed all week and aren't part of the plan: The Diamond Horseshoe, the Walt Disney World Railroad's Frontierland stop, and Walt Disney's Carousel of Progress.

7:00 PM — Level 99 at Disney Springs (Premium tickets, 2.5 hours, party of 3, reservation code JFWWSP) — this is where Alex joins the trip. His flight lands at MCO at 4:01 PM (AA #YLVTNE), giving him plenty of time to get to Disney Springs on his own before your 7 PM start. MK's 6 PM closure hands you two a clean window to make the drive over and get settled in first — running till around 9:30 PM.

Every Level99 player needs their own account with a signed waiver before arrival — easiest done from home ahead of the 15th rather than at the welcome desk that night.
Animal Kingdom is the one gate park with no day of its own on this trip — nothing on your must-do list points there, so it's sitting out this round rather than getting squeezed in.

Disney's promo art for The Magic of Disney Animation — each "department" is themed to a different film (Alice in Wonderland, Frozen, Moana, Toy Story, Zootopia, and more).

For Mike — what this is: a brand-new walk-through experience that just opened Sept 14, 2026 (right at the start of your trip) in Animation Courtyard, replacing the old Magic of Disney Animation building. It's built like an "open house" at Disney's animation studio: you wander through galleries and character "offices" themed to different films, watch the Emmy-winning short Once Upon a Studio in a dedicated theater, catch character meet-and-greets (Mulan, Rapunzel, Stitch and others) staged as animation-department scenes, and there's an Olaf-hosted drawing class if you want to try sketching a character yourself. There's also a padded indoor play area themed to Mary Blair's Alice in Wonderland art, geared toward little kids — probably one to skip for your group. All in, worth budgeting 45–75 minutes.

New HS restaurants? Nothing new is actually open yet. Harryhausen's — an upscale sushi spot themed to the original Monsters, Inc., landing in the new Monstropolis land — was just announced but isn't slated to open until 2027. Nothing to book for the 17th; worth keeping in mind for a future trip.
